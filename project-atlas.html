The whole tech stack, one consistent partner

Atlas Training Center is an early childhood education organization with staff, parents, and educators all relying on day-to-day technology that just has to work. Rather than juggling four or five different vendors, they wanted one technical partner to own the whole stack.

That's where I come in — managing everything from the web hosting and email infrastructure to the learning management system, database, and backup processes that keep the lights on.

What I handle

  • Web hosting — uptime monitoring, performance tuning, DNS and SSL
  • LMS administration and database management for the curriculum platform
  • Automated backup handling with verified restore procedures
  • Email hosting and ongoing inbox / domain management
  • Day-to-day IT support — hardware, accounts, troubleshooting

Outcome

Atlas's staff can focus on running the center, not on managing technology. When something does need attention, there's one point of contact who already knows the full setup — which means faster resolutions and fewer surprises.
